# Album 113 — Heavens Declare (வானங்கள் அறிவிக்கின்றன)
Enoch, Elijah, and the Coming City — Album 113

Nine songs on the heavens that declare. Enoch walked with God. Elijah’s whirlwind. Third heaven vision. Ten heavens noted as non-canonical. Pillar of cloud and fire. New Jerusalem coming. Adoil as vision only. Maranatha. The heavens still declare. Tamil and English. Sense kept.

# Track List:
1. ENOCH WALKED WITH GOD.
2. ELIJAH’S WHIRLWIND.
3. THIRD HEAVEN VISION.
4. TEN HEAVENS NOTED.
5. PILLAR OF CLOUD AND FIRE.
6. NEW JERUSALEM COMING.
7. ADOIL AS VISION ONLY.
8. MARANATHA, COME LORD JESUS.
9. VAANAMUM BOOMIYUM.
